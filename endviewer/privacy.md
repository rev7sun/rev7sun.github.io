# endviewer Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how endviewer collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the endviewer Discord application.

endviewer is a Discord application for Clash of Clans player, Legend League, battlelog, clan, scouting, and related analytics features.

Contact: @rev7sun on Discord or [join the support Discord!](https://discord.gg/Z9vAYw5ZJs)

## 1. Information endviewer Collects

endviewer collects only the information needed to provide and maintain its features.

### Discord Information

endviewer may collect:

- Discord user IDs for users who run commands or configure tracked/watchlisted players.
- Discord guild/server IDs where the bot is used.
- Discord channel IDs where tracking or command features are configured.
- Discord interaction IDs, command names, command options, custom button/select IDs, and similar interaction metadata.
- Discord usernames or tags in development/error logs when an interaction is slow or fails.

endviewer does not intentionally collect message contents outside of slash command inputs and Discord interaction data sent to the bot.

### Clash of Clans Information

endviewer may collect or cache:

- Clash of Clans player tags and clan tags submitted to commands.
- Player names, clan names, trophy counts, Legend League battle details, battle timestamps, opponent tags, stars, destruction percentages, army share codes, league history, clan history, rankings, and related game data returned by the official Clash of Clans API or configured third-party data providers.
- Day trophy cache values, including estimated or reconstructed start/current trophy values.
- Battlelog cache rows used to make recent or historical endviewer summaries work.

Most Clash of Clans data used by endviewer is game-related data available through public or authenticated Clash of Clans data APIs.

### Configuration and Usage Information

endviewer may store:

- Watchlist and tracking settings, including player tags, groups, labels, whether opponent details should be cached, and who added the entry.
- Known battle IDs used to avoid duplicate tracking.
- Command usage counts by command name.
- Runtime logs for errors, slow interactions, and service issues.

### Information From Third-Party Providers

When configured, endviewer may request information from third-party Clash of Clans data providers, including ClashKing. Those providers may receive the request information needed to answer the query, such as a Clash of Clans player tag, clan tag, Discord guild ID, role ID, or API request metadata.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 2. How endviewer Uses Information

endviewer uses collected information to:

- Respond to Discord slash commands and interactions.
- Generate Clash of Clans summaries, scouting output, and player analytics.
- Maintain watchlists and tracking configuration.
- Cache battlelog and trophy data so later summaries can work when live APIs no longer return older data.
- Avoid duplicate battle notifications or repeated processing.
- Diagnose errors, performance issues, and abuse.
- Maintain basic command usage statistics.
- Keep the bot reliable and secure.

endviewer does not sell personal information.

## 3. Legal Basis and Consent

By using endviewer or configuring it in a Discord server, you consent to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy.

Server administrators who add endviewer to a server are responsible for making sure the bot's use is appropriate for that server.

## 4. How Long Information Is Kept

endviewer keeps information for as long as needed to provide its features, operate the bot, debug problems, or comply with legal and safety obligations.

Some cached data may be kept until it is manually deleted, overwritten, or removed by an administrative cleanup command. Local development instances may store data in JSON files. Hosted instances may store data in a database such as Postgres.

endviewer's current cache behavior may keep up to 2,000 battlelog entries per cached player, plus day trophy cache rows and watch/tracking rows until they are removed.

## 5. How Information Is Shared

endviewer may share information in these limited ways:

- With Discord, because endviewer operates inside Discord and responds through Discord APIs.
- With the official Clash of Clans API, when game data is requested.
- With configured third-party Clash of Clans data providers, such as ClashKing, when their data is needed for a feature.
- With hosting, database, logging, or infrastructure providers used to run endviewer.
- With server users in command responses, embeds, buttons, or other Discord bot output.
- If required by law, legal process, security needs, or to protect the rights and safety of users, servers, the bot, or others.

endviewer does not sell collected information.

## 6. Public Command Output

Command results may be visible to other people in the Discord channel where the command is used, unless Discord or the bot marks the response as private/ephemeral.

Do not submit information to endviewer commands if you do not want it to appear in bot responses or logs.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Bot tokens, API tokens, database credentials, and other secrets should never be posted publicly or shared in Discord messages.

## 8. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

You can stop new collection in a server by no longer using endviewer commands or by removing endviewer from the server.

You may request deletion of stored data associated with a Clash of Clans player tag, Discord user ID, server, or channel by contacting:

@rev7sun on Discord or [join the support Discord!](https://discord.gg/Z9vAYw5ZJs)

endviewer also includes owner/admin cleanup tools that can preview and delete stored data for player tags, including cached battlelog rows, watched players, tracked players, and day trophy cache rows.

We may need enough information to verify and complete a deletion request, such as the relevant Discord user ID, server ID, channel ID, or Clash of Clans player tag.

Some information may remain in backups, provider logs, security logs, or Discord messages outside our direct control.

## 9. Children's Privacy

endviewer is intended for use within Discord and Clash of Clans communities. We do not knowingly collect information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information that should be removed, contact us using the contact method above.

## 10. International Users

endviewer may be hosted or operated in countries different from your own. By using endviewer, you understand that information may be processed where the bot, hosting provider, database, Discord, Supercell, or third-party data providers operate.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be effective when posted unless it says otherwise.
